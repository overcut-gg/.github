# Overcut

**Browser-first sim racing telemetry.** Live data from your simulator on any
screen: a tablet on the desk, a phone, a second monitor. No app to install on
the device you are watching from.

**→ [overcut.gg](https://overcut.gg)**

### What it does

Overcut reads live telemetry from **Assetto Corsa Competizione** and
**Gran Turismo 7**, streams it to any browser on your network, and after the
session shows corner by corner where your lap time went.

- **Live dashboard** on any screen, with no desktop app on the viewing device
- **Lap analysis**: sector deltas, theoretical best, per-corner attribution
- **Remote coaching**: share a live session by link, no account needed to watch
- **OBS overlays** for streamers
- **Local-first**: raw telemetry is processed on your own PC and stays there

### About this organisation

Overcut is led by **Felipe Martins** and operated from Poland. This organisation
was created on **30 April 2026** and the product has operated under the Overcut
name since. Its creation timestamp is readable from the public GitHub API at
`api.github.com/orgs/overcut-gg`.

The Overcut Connector is closed source and distributed as a code-signed Windows
build from [overcut.gg/download](https://overcut.gg/download). Full provenance,
with the records each date comes from, is on
[overcut.gg/about](https://overcut.gg/about).
